        type orig.css extra.css > index-vXXX.css   (Windows)  /  cat orig.css extra.css > index-vXXX.css
Optional: gzip -9k both files (server sends .gz automatically when present).
Copy into ..\web\assets\ and point ..\web\index.html at the new names.
